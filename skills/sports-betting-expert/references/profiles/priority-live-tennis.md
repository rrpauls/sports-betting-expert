# Profile: priority-live-tennis

This optional profile controls the screening order. Apply it only after explicit activation and stop applying it if the user changes the preference. The canonical skill's live-only tennis gate applies whether or not this profile is active.

## Screening order

For broad daily pre-match searches, screen football first, baseball second and cricket third. Consider basketball and ice hockey when they offer better evidence and price. American football, volleyball, handball and other sports remain conditional on data quality and market coverage. Do not force a selection merely to represent a sport; prefer two to four supported legs over a larger marginal accumulator.

## Cricket emphasis

Treat Test, ODI and T20 separately. T20 usually needs a larger uncertainty allowance and may be better suited to live, powerplay or innings markets. Always condition analysis on venue, toss, weather, pitch, batting depth, bowling roles and chase/dew effects.
